What does “expected score advantage” mean?

Our final score for this game minus the opponents’ final score, averaged over the possible outcomes. Our −160 against their 215 gives −375; against their 250 it gives −410. The first result is 35 points better for us. The expectation is a belief-based prediction: exact scoring rules are applied to uncertain outcomes, then weighted by their estimated probabilities. These are game scores under the rules, including contract rewards and penalties—not just captured card points.
